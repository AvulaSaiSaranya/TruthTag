TRUTHTAG TRANSPARENCY CHARTER

Understanding Digital Verification Limitations

Why an authentic database entry can never replace physical packaging forensics.

The Reality of Serial & QR Cloning

Counterfeiting operations routinely purchase one legitimate retail product, photograph its genuine QR matrix and serial number, and print identical duplicates onto 10,000 counterfeit packages.

Therefore, a positive database match simply confirms that the identifier was legitimately registered by the brand. It does not prove that the specific box in your possession is the original factory product.

"TruthTag guarantees transparent record inspection—not magical certainty. The best defense is combining ledger verification with physical inspection."

The 4-Point Physical Inspection Guide

  • ✓
    Examine Tamper Seals: Check for genuine holographic void labels that leave honeycomb residues when peeled.
  • ✓
    Check Micro-Typography: Fake packaging often displays blurred micro-print, misaligned margins, or uneven ink saturation.
  • ✓
    Audit the Retail Chain: Was this purchased from an authorized brand retailer or an unauthorized third-party vendor at a 75% discount?
  • ✓
    What to do if Inconclusive: Contact the brand's support desk with your serial number before using or consuming the product.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about product provenance, barcode security, and threat models.

Barcodes and QR codes are just open strings of text converted into black-and-white graphics. Anyone with an office printer can scan a real shoe box or perfume bottle and replicate that barcode on thousands of fake cartons. Digital matching verifies that the code is valid in the ledger, but physical security features are essential to confirm the box is real.

Inconclusive means the code was not found in the current ledger. This happens when: (1) there is a typo in the serial number, (2) the brand has not enrolled this regional batch, or (3) the item is an unauthorized duplicate. We recommend verifying the code with the retailer or manufacturer customer service before using the product.

TruthTag Privacy & Verification Charter

1. Client-Side Data Isolation

TruthTag operates entirely within your browser runtime. All verification history entries, registered sample products, and telemetry calculations are stored securely in browser localStorage. No search queries or IP addresses are uploaded to external tracking servers.

2. Verification Scope & Limitations

A positive record match indicates that the submitted identifiers (SKU, Lot, Serial Number) correspond to an authentic registered production batch in our ledger. It does not certify that a physical tag has not been duplicated onto a counterfeit item.

3. Fictional Demo Sandbox

All brand entities (AuraSound, Vitalis Laboratories, AeroStride, NordicShield, OmniPower, Chronos Horology) are fictional representations designed exclusively to demonstrate authentic vs. warning vs. inconclusive workflows.
